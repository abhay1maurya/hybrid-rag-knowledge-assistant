Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Hybrid RAG Knowledge Assistant, offline modes, and data privacy.

General Information

Unlike standard RAG systems, our platform allows you to seamlessly toggle between powerful cloud-based LLMs for deep, complex reasoning and completely local, offline models for maximum privacy and zero-latency document retrieval.

Currently, Hybrid RAG supports PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown files. We automatically parse text, tables, and basic formatting to ensure the AI understands the full context of your uploads.

Technical & Offline Mode

Offline mode downloads quantized versions of open-source language models directly to your machine. Once downloaded, all document embedding, vector searching, and answer generation happen entirely on your local hardware without sending a single byte over the internet.

No! While a dedicated GPU (like an NVIDIA RTX series) significantly speeds up response times, our assistant is highly optimized. Thanks to advanced model quantization, you can run the offline inference efficiently on modern multi-core CPUs.

We continually update our model registry. Currently, we support optimized, locally-runnable versions of Meta's Llama 3, Mistral, and Google's Gemma models, available in varying parameter sizes depending on your hardware limits.

The base application requires minimal space (under 500MB). However, downloading the local AI models will require additional storage. A standard 7B parameter quantized model typically requires between 4GB to 6GB of free disk space.

Yes. If you upload a PDF consisting only of scanned images, our pipeline utilizes an integrated Optical Character Recognition (OCR) step to extract the raw text before passing it to the vector database for RAG processing.

Privacy & Security

Absolutely not. We prioritize enterprise-grade security. In Offline AI mode, your documents literally never leave your computer. In online mode, we utilize isolated data containers via enterprise APIs, explicitly opting out of any data sharing for LLM training purposes.

We use industry-standard JWT (JSON Web Tokens) for secure, stateless user sessions. All connections to our online services are protected by strict TLS encryption, ensuring your queries and document uploads are completely secure in transit.

When utilizing the cloud processing features, your files are securely stored in encrypted AWS cloud storage buckets. Data is localized to specific geographic regions to comply with local data residency laws.

Only you and your authorized workspace members. Our platform employs strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Our engineering and support teams cannot view, access, or query your uploaded proprietary data.

Yes. You retain full ownership and control over your data. If you delete a document, a workspace, or your entire account, all associated raw files and vector embeddings are permanently wiped from our servers immediately.
